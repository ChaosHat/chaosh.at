## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-25 15.08.23.webp]]

*Ichiban has love for the OGs*

Let's talk mechanics a bit. I like combat, it's very fast, even boss fights haven't felt like they overstay their welcome. There are a lot of things to like about the positioning combat and the weaknesses but I don't feel the game gives you gives you enough ability to interact with it. I understand the sequel lets you move units in combat and that's a welcome change. Right now it's a little annoying, for some aoe moves you're incentivized to wait until they line themselves up and that's time consuming.

I finished chapter three, an interesting ending there. I'm surprised they tried to zag on me with the "Nanoha wanted him dead" since I initially thought that WAS the case, she was paying for someone to kill her dad so she wouldn't be burdened by him. But then they immediately resolved that. I was bummed to see the soapland guy dead, he really did seem to care and be decent. Owning and operating the now available soapland seems like a thing the party could/would do. 

![[2026-11-25 15.31.34.webp]]

The dungeon design is something that was a little perplexing. They're pretty linear but they manage to work in the backtracking for items which is a jrpg staple I don't love. It was also weird to get a key for a locked door that led to not the boss room but instead a room with blueprints of the facility that led to a secret ladder in the room you just came from. Also what's with the silver safes? They always ask if I want to open them, is there a reason to not?

I'm happy to get a female character in the party, although Saeko joined with the least preamble. In combat I kind of struggle to see what her purpose is. She's very frail and doesn't deal much damage. Her buffing and debuffing seem not especially worth it since the fights are so fast. We'll see if I'm missing something. 

![[2026-11-25 15.52.32.webp]]

I finally also got weapon crafting which was nice, I knew this was coming because the game keeps throwing all these materials at you. Also happy to have another prominent reoccurring female character. Unfortunately the upgraded baseball bat was still not more powerful than the Hitachi Magic Wand. Which makes sense. It's a *magic wand.* I wouldn't expect a baseball bat to beat Harry Potter. 

There do seem to be quite a few systems and minigames here and they largely seem optional but I have issues keeping them straight. Like there was the hangout at the bar and I could get more bond, can I keep doing it? My bond also seems locked, they haven't increased in a while and I assume it's waiting for plot beats to advance. And then there's the can driving and the vocational school. I definitely feel cash restricted on interacting with some of it. 

Spent an absolute ton of time catching up on some subquests. Or actually just learning how to get them. I guess when I started talking to randos it was just some random text and I stopped, but one of the little achievement/mission things was for talking to people. So I started and that unlocked it. The subquests are super addictive. Just do one more step. One more. One more.
