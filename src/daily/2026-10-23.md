We beat up a Ramuh today, huzzah! Added him to the team. The fight was tough but not too bad. 

I'm not totally sure on what my strategy with the visions should be, especially now that I have more than enough for the party. Early on I just kind of round robin'd it so everyone would max out everyone because well, what else were they going to do? But now it feels like I have to consider a build and such. 

![[20261023221527_1.jpg]]

Did a bunch more fights and worked on some side questing before trekking on to the Wind Shrine. Once there, we defeated Aether, whose post fight reveal was spoiled seconds before the fight started for those genre savvy enough to pick it up. I went about the island and wrapped up some stuff, grabbing the Onion Knight vision before we left on the airship. That officially ended chapter 2, Eternal Blue.

![[20261023223514_1.jpg]]

*She said the name of the chapter!*

Plot feels like it's picking up a bit. Did some exploring of the next zone before going to bed, found a lead on my next esper. Was a bit underleveled coming into this zone, the trash mobs took a dramatic step up after being pretty easy on the prior area. Although maybe that was my fault, I kept escaping from fights to level up that Chocobo Kick power. 

The characters are overall pretty charming if standard. I'm really interested to see how Fina evolves back into that Archwitch. I am enjoying these Mechabo upgrades and the NPC who does them. He seems suitably insane.
