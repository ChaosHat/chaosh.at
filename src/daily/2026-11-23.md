## Bad Blood
Yes books! I probably won't write daily on them because my rule is literally just read one page a day and that daily habit has largely stuck. Some days it's more when I'm feeling it! But minimum one. So I'll just write about these when I have something to write about. 

So probably like a bunch of people, I decided to read this after You Can See Everything. I'm about halfway now and it really dispels a lot of what image rehab Holmes was attempting with the movie. She is a terrible person who really thought reality would bend to her will if she just wanted it enough. There are some things she could maybe try to throw other people, namely Sunny, under the bus for but there's so much that she specifically did that is *awful.* Like everything with the scientist she drove into depression and suicide. 
## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Remake)
![[20261123225504-01M85MP043X7Q6Z75634Y8V2VV.jpg]]

*Piss off, ghosts!*

Got through the Forest Temple and well, it's mostly the same. There was a difference in the final Poe fight, the flames changed color on the first two and I couldn't figure out what the difference was on the third, I just got lucky and shot the correct one on the second try. 

The game overall does seem to be a lot stingier on ammo and I wonder if that's to make the business scrubs and the new summoning seed more valuable. The dropped arrows are in fewer quantities and there have been multiple times where I need bombs in a way I never did before. I had to actually buy some from a shop at one point. 

![[20261123225512-01M85MP2H6KZAD8WH2YW3REGTK.webp]]

*Even more adorable!*

The Phantom Ganon fight was marginally harder because the camera defaults to behind Link instead of the N64 version panned it out to the side it seemed. Slightly annoying. Also I don't recall if he had that charge move before or if I just killed him too quickly in the last run. 

They did absolutely cuteify the Great Deku Sprout. Also when we first returned to Kokiri village as adult Link my daughter asked why Link grew up but the other Kokiri didn't. I told her that the Great Deku Tree told us about how Link's mom came to the forest and blah blah. Well I guess I wasn't paying super good attention during that cutscene because it's actually the sprout that says that! I'm glad she didn't call me out on it. 
## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261122-223531.webp]]

*Spooky.*

Finished the Kalm retelling, the Rebirth version really does a better job conveying Tifa's uncertainty even before she straight up tells Aerith that Cloud wasn't at Nibelheim five years ago. Again, the benefit of being able to use tone in voice over and also higher fidelity of mannerisms and such. 

Caught the Chocobo which is actually much more annoying than the Rebirth method! Forcing me to spend 2k gil on the materia and then also buying the greens was a big oof. I had to grind out some gil to do it. Once you have all that, the Chocobos run away pretty quickly, I had one leave when I was trying to choose the item and target it, and another after it ate the first greens. 

Not fighting the Midgarsormr is something I'm not sure about. It's a bit of a violation of Chekov's Gun. You see the big monster but you're never forced to confront it and see how scary, Sephiroth just handles it for you. Maybe that's okay because in the flashback you could see how powerful he was? I also suppose some players might have met a game over trying to fight it without getting the chocobo first.
