## sausage
You might have noticed some rapid font changes on the site and that's because I've been trying to fix some things. One thing they never tell you about pixel fonts is how annoying they are! Pixel fonts have to scale in whole numbers to look crisp and not blurry (so you have to go from 1x to 2x in size) and for a while I was hacking it with a 16.5 px font but I got irritated at how often that looked blurry. 

So the answer has been to try some fonts out and get hacky with it a bit. This size is too small, this size is too big, what if we make the small one bold? It's very annoying because I'm very picky with both the blurriness and the site trying to look cohesive and not a mishmash of fonts. 

If you're thinking about designing a website, maybe don't do pixels. It's a little annoying!

Although one thing that IS a pain in the butt is how JRPGs are so linear but offer forking paths for the explicit purpose of hiding something there. So then you have to check each and every nook in case there's a cool thing to find there and sometimes there isn't. And they definitely get you with some situations where you're not sure which fork to go down and you get the primary path and then you have to backtrack to the secrets path and then go down the primary path. They're not even clever it's just "here's a chest at the end of this path with a dead end."

Some interesting plot drops here. If I had to guess it seems like this whole dual personality thing is also part of the Sworn Six, and it's possible that Fina, in her Archwitch persona, is/was one of them but who broke off. What's a little confusing here is that it seemed like The First Cid was a bad dude, or maybe a good dude who was doing bad things for reasons but Alluvion was definitely pretty bad, then snapped out of it and had to come to terms with it. Cid never seemed the same. He felt maybe more like a Felix to steal a reference from Golden Sun. I have some theories about the Sworn Six, one of them being ||Rain's dad||. 

I think the odds of Fina being some kind of trickster bad guy who would betray the protagonists went way down here. Also quite touching with, uh, I want to say El? I did think he was going to save the crystal for a bit. Also a real gut punch with Arsha when we got back. I mean I expected that but still dang.
