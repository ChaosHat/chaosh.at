## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2909400_20261110123827_1.webp]]

*This is a waste of my time.*

I'm a cheater. I went and downloaded some mods to make the open world stuff easier. I just don't see the value in some of it, the qtes and such. Part of what feels bad about it is how slow and clunky some of it is. There are lots of nice animations and menus but after the first time you've seen it, it gets old. Like the huge fanfare after you scan the lifestream. Okay cool but can I move on and go? 

![[2909400_20261110125459_1.webp]]

*Cloud's dainty SOLDIER butt.*

This is all over the place too. Like the cushions for the rest stops. First of all, it's insane that you need one. Cloud, the tough SOLDIER First Class, won't sit on a bench without a cushion? Secondly, the animation of the whole thing is nice the first time you see it, but every time? 

![[2909400_20261110130359_1 1.webp]]

*This is pretty but we could move on faster.*

Things like Queen's Blood I really like, or Ft. Condor, these are mostly nice change of pace things. But I don't need to fight a bunch of extra mobs in the world intel thing. Or round up the Moogles every time. 
## Sovereign Tower
Well I went and got Edith possessed by the demon in her sword. It's probably fine. The demon said Edith thanks me. 

I beat this and it was pretty good. The world building here is really good, it's witty, and the art style is simple but charming. I really liked the writing and characters and how the plot weaves. I can tell there are all these different paths and options and it feels like I've seen maybe 20% of all the little interactions and story paths. 

Unfortunately, I don't think I'll be replaying it to see them. 

The third act takes too long and doesn't have enough interesting other stuff to pull you through. The other acts were shorter and had big signpost things to prepare for at the end. Without that, the quests rapidly become samey and the base gameplay isn't enough to inspire me to replay. This isn't a dopamine machine like a Slay the Spire where I'd be happy to just keep playing for the sake of it. I think I could just read what happens and that would be enough. 

I think a sequel that addresses this would be an all time game and I'm glad I played it. For most people, I'd say this is a wait for a sale game. 
## The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess
Alright, I started playing this some. Why? I have no idea. It wasn't on my radar at all, I was just looking for something to do after Sovereign Tower and remembered that I had set up Dusklight a while ago. 

I played for about an hour and got kind of lost in the figuring out of where to go and what to do in the exact sequence for the early game. I kept trying to herd the cat back over because I thought I needed the slingshot to hit the monkey to get the cradle but no. You use the hawk to hit the monkey to get the cradle to get the fishing rod to get the cat to get the slingshot.

Did all that, got the sword, got the kid freed from the monkey jail. It's fun. I'm not sure I'll do the whole thing. It is on my mind that I should be playing Prince of Persia but for some reason that seems technical enough that I can't just pick it up casually. Also it's a much more strict saving experience, I can't just pop in and out. I'm not going to set this to active yet, we'll see if it gets more play.
