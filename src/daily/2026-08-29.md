## chrono
I was really concerned if I'd like this because I bounced off FFVI so hard but that seems like much less of a concern here. Lucca and Marle seem like much more interesting characters and they're not portrayed so... seriously. The entire thing doesn't take itself seriously, it's clearly trying to be funny. And to be fair, FFVI was funny, but it just gave some severe whiplash, melodrama to absurdity and back again. 

The combat is also better. Timing (I get it) being an aspect beyond just atb is really good! 

## kh1
Started this today, Lily's been asking to watch me play it. I got it on steam sale a bit ago, I've never played it. 

The intro is the most early 2000s ass thing of all time. The character designs, the music, the cool twilight lettering. 

The disney characters look so lovely and Sora looks like a fucking nightmare. 

Game is sooooo basic and soooooo slowly paced.
