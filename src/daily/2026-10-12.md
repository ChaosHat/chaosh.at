I think I've committed to holding off on Galactic Racer until it goes on sale. Now, Sovereign Tower is on sale...
## smrpg
Got Bowser, so this is officially the farthest I've ever been in this game. I think I was already well past that with Moleville but I know I've gotten Geno and I've never gotten Bowser. 

It's fun, but silly. So far Geno is cool but he's made of glass and keeps dying. I've been using Bowser over Geno just because he's a thick Koopa King. Peach is a pretty amazing healer but she's extremely one note but she heals so efficiently it's hard to not use her I think. I'd say you definitely need her or Mallow and Mallow is more well balanced, but the AOE heal and cleanse effect are so strong. 

This Booster series of events is so weird and fever dream like. It's just unhinged from start to finish. The sunken ship nearly broke me though. Sooooo many fights all the time. Good lord. I think it's a good thing this game is so short I'm not sure the gameplay is strong enough to be invested in the long haul. 
## sovereign tower
Alright I did get this and god damn what a "one more turn" game this is. Addictive. It really put a smile on my face when you use the time travel effect and the game accounts for that and you can forsee the future in your conversations.

It's an rpg where you play as the king who is chosen by this tower to rule and you basically take audiences from people who need things and then send your knights out to do stuff, make decisions, try to manage the kingdom. It's kind of like a first person Civ game kind of. You hear people during the morning, assign your knights to quests, hope they do well and then you do it again!

I did let a knight die. That was a bummer, but I really don't want to abuse the time travel aspect because you know...*it's a demon*. That doesn't sound prudent.
