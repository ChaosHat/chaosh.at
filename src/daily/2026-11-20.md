## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2026-11-20 13.03.10.webp]]

*Beach ready.*

I finished off all the companion card games and there's no achievement?! Why did I get all of them then? If we rank companion card games I'd say: Royal Concert > Pirate's Rampage > Run Wild > Card Carnival > Wheelie Rendezvous > Cactuar Caper. I don't like the puzzles as much as regular Queen's Blood, and Piano is just fun. I would play more of basically any of the top three. Wheelie Rendezvous wins slightly because of how unhinged Jenny is watching you put the wheelies back.

I find it very touching how much the crew cares about the robed guys. I dunno why Aerith decides to let Hojo go as "we've done enough." No Aerith, we haven't *killed him*. He needs to die. 

![[2026-11-20 13.21.04.webp]]

I really love the specific way this game looks great. The fact that everything is done in engine so the cutscenes have the actual equipment and look that you've set them up with, and how seamless it is to go from gameplay to those cutscenes. When they do it in the middle of combat it's so fluid. 

Post Hojo, the chapter ends and we finally get Yuffie added to the party. I wish the game didn't require Cloud to be a part of literally every party so I could have two different teams of three to rotate out for overworld stuff. I'm pretty sure it's not optimal but I may roll with Cloud/Tifa/Yuffie, they're the characters that I think have the most interesting combat mechanics. As good as Aerith is as a caster I never actually like controlling her in combat. 
## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-20 21.29.25.webp]]

*Excited to see how this guy comes back into the game.*
 
I gotta say, I'm pretty floored by how much I'm vibing with this game. The characters are continually interesting, I want to get to know them more, they have unique little edges and well...character. Nanba, the ex cop, the businessman all have this air of mystery. Not to mention the stuff you know a bit more about. Ichiban is so charismatic, you want him to succeed. What's going on with Arakawa? How could he turn on Ichiban?

I mean, I get why Ichiban has to be persona non grata, now he allegedly killed a guy for Arakawa's new family. It is curious why he sent Ichiban that letter though. **ased** on the timeline that should have been after Arakawa did the flip and knew he wouldn't be able to see Ichiban? It does feel bad to see Ichiban so punished for his trust in Arakawa, but on the other hand he really seems to be repaid for his trust in most everyone else. 

![[2026-11-20 21.53.53.webp]]

*Finally, some revenge on this asshole.*

They zagged on me a bit with Arakawa too. I thought he was going to bite it and this was going to be some kind of revenge fueled rampage for Ichiban. And well, it still might be, but not for that. 

The game also finds little touching moments. I'm not sure why but Ichiban's interactions with the cop who finds him sleeping before he turns him in were so gutting. The guy was so nice, I'm sure it hurt Ichiban to let him down, even though he just met him, didn't know him. Or the oddly kind prison officer giving him a pep talk. 

![[2026-11-20 22.22.48.webp]]

*This game somehow manages to walk a tightrope of tone where this fits.*

I can't believe this random prison officer is giving Ichiban a pep talk. Absolutely gutting that he pours his heart out and Arakawa isn't there. 

Combat seems to lave a lot of unexplained context and did get a bit deeper with some party members. Moving past people matters, it's not like Dragon Quest where the positioning is irrelevant. Also, hitting from behind or on the ground matters a lot. Some of the location contextual stuff would be more interesting if you could control where you are.
