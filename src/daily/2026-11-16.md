## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
![[2026-11-16 12.00.06.webp]]

*So sisterly.*

The Spirit Temple completed, this one is pretty refreshingly straight forward overall, although there is a weird part right at the end that I got mixed up and had to backtrack a bit (but I didn't actually have to). It's right after you lower the platform below the boss chamber, I remembered that you have to burn off the face with the mirror shield. Once you do that there is a grate in front of the entrance and there's no indication of how to handle that. All you have to do is longshot over and it moves out of the way, but to my knowledge, the game never uses any design language to say "you can hookshot to this." It seems like you should have to open the grate beforehand. I circled around a bit before realizing this. 

![[2026-11-16 12.02.05.webp]]

*The design on this character is pretty cool.*

I like the Twinrova fight overall, it's one of the more interesting versions of "use the thing from the dungeon to kill the boss." They probably could have made a more elaborate fight that incorporated bombchus and the big ol' gauntlet. I did initially forget how to handle the second phase. I remembered you had to absorb with the shield but I couldn't remember the triggering step after that. Did you have to absorb the other element to trigger it? No, it's the classic "do a thing three times."

![[2026-11-16 12.14.26.webp]]

*Who could this mysterious person be?*

All that said, the second phase is again the kind of thing where it just becomes boring. You wait for her to send the right color, walk to the side if it's the wrong one. 

Also, Nabooru is definitely the sage who did the least to deserve it right? From a Link connection perspective she just rolls up at the last minute. I suppose she spent seven years being tortured. Alright you're in the club. 

![[2026-11-16 12.14.43 1.webp]]

*Egads, it's Zelda! I'm shocked!*

Afterwards, this is where my QOL adjustments hurt me since the Song of Time no longer teleports me to the Temple of Time, it just changes my age right then and there. I ended up teleporting to Kakariko and Epona-ing over to the Temple. A quick chat with Sheik/Zelda and we're off to the end game. 

It's bizarre that they've never done a Zelda/Sheik follow up? That seems like an easy idea. You could just do it in the seven years between the eras of Ocarina, showing her training and what was she even training for? Why do you need to be a ninja? There's probably a cool story about her setting up for Link's return, figuring out who the sages are, learning the songs, doing her best to stop Ganon's forces. Sheik was obviously super popular in Smash (obviously some of that was her strength).
## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
I had about thirty minutes to kill and I figured I could accomplish something relevant in this! And I did! We got that new chocobo quest done and a few world intel things along the way. I guess the world intel stuff isn't that bad after all. 

I just love chocobos for some reason. They're awesome and cute and the theme song probably goes a long way. It's a real ear worm, I'll catch myself just whistling it a bunch. It's up there with Saria's Song for catchy game music.
