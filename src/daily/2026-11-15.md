## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
![[Screenshot_20261115-092446.webp]]

*Sorry Bongo Bongo, I'm already familiar with your game.*

Wrapped up the Shadow Temple, it's not a bad temple overall. Remembering that the Sun Song does things to the redead helps a lot. Also why is there a boat in the middle of this thing? There are some irritating elements, for the life of me those spinning scythe things are so annoying. Also there was a hallway where I kept running into surprise Skulltulas like Sideshow Bob with rakes. Bongo Bongo is another fight that's just absolutely trivial if you know what's up. 

I've given up on being very completionist on this run, I'm just going to do that on the remake. I decided this as the shadow temple boat sailed past a skulltula and was like nah, I'm not getting that. Granted, I'm this far and I only have just shy of thirty so that wasn't really in the cards anyways. 

![[Screenshot_20261115-101353.webp]]

*I love the way these carpenter dudes run away.*

The Gerudo Valley Thieves' Hideout is so annoying. I spent so long trying to find this fourth carpenter. I went in the correct room a bunch of times but that hallway just looked like another one I thought it was the same. I also went through it like Rambo, just arrowing Gerudo women left and right. 

After I eventually found the last one, it was off to the desert where it took me an embarrassing number of attempts to get past the quicksand. I followed the ghost and got to the temple, learned a new song and Ocarina'd my way through time. 
## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Remake)
![[20261115165310-01M7GJEV3M7T9HMBRV2WK20080.webp]]

*After this conversation ends she just bails on me until morning instead of teaching me the dang song.* 

Another King Dodongo defeated. But, en route, we went to Lon Lon Ranch to get my bottle and Epona's Song. The passage of time happening in town zones is not an improvement, Malon went inside mid conversation so I had to wait until daytime again to actually learn the song. 

Speaking of not an improvement, the way items work in remake is pretty annoying. In the original you could equip three items to face buttons, now you just have one. Yes, it is easier to switch them out via the quick menu but it's not better than it used to be. I'm truly spoiled in Ship of Harkinian, you get to bind items to the dpad too. 

Also a weird thing, I always thought Navi being a point of light was just a stylistic thing, she glowed but had a human like form, you know, like the great fairies. But she apparently just is a fuzzy ball?

On the way through Kakariko to Death Mountain I went to get the Sun's Song and died which was annoying. I didn't respect the Redead enough, two hits and that was it. They were shockingly healthy. 

A couple things felt impossible to do without the bomb bag, like tossing the bomb in the giant Goron vase for the heart pieces or trying to stop the rolling guy. There were just fewer bomb flower locations in Goron City. 

![[20261115165256-01M7GJF28B4JPPYNGXR3W0WJQ7.webp]]

*Much less awkward sounding and looking.*

Dodongo Cavern felt very straightforward and I'm not opposed to the streamlining. I think a lot of people complaining about simplification are giving themselves too much credit. None of this was ever that hard. 

King Dodongo himself did seem a bit harder, I don't recall him spawning the babies and that was an additional wrinkle that interrupted the flow of the fight. I had to handle them and stay out of the blast radius of their corpses. 

Finished up with the Great Fairy and grabbing a few things in Kakariko Village that I could do now that I had bombs. Speaking of, I've also been using the Zelda notes app, it's pretty slick and useful. I enjoy knowing where all the things are although maybe it could tell me if I'm actually capable of accessing them now.
