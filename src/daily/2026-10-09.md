It might be time for a break from the Weave. I might put it down for a while or something or just see. I think it's mostly just fatigue and just a need for novelty. And I've thought I might be done at least a couple times during this but like it might actually be time. There's more writing about my frustrations below but I wanted to lead with some dabbling. 

Part of my problem now though is I have no idea what I actually want to play. This was all consuming and I'm not sure what feels good. I do kind of want to play more Fire Emblem so I could try a romhack or Engage or something. 
## dwm2
I played this on a recommendation because I felt like an easy, chill little rpg and god damn the waves of nostalgia for something I never played originally. That game boy look just is so classic, the fonts and the way characters look in these kinds of games. 

I dabbled a bit, maybe 45 minutes but I think this game is too simple for me to really continue playing, to be honest what I'm probably looking for is a Pokemon (possibly romhack?) but this had me thinking maybe I should play more GB/C games. That leads to...

## fefw
I've spent a ton of time on this specific fight (part 3, chapter 2) and it's challenging. I think it's still fun challenging but I'm kind of over it and it's hard to tell if it's not fun and I'm frustrated or the general irritation with the game is bleeding into an otherwise solid fight. One thing that definitely IS frustrating is that the fights get so long! I think I'm about to have to restart it due to a lack of sand to get me over the hump and I believe that's about 20 turns of painstakingly moving forward to not get overwhelmed down the drain. 

Part of me thinks I should just reduce the difficulty and power through but then that gets into my whole complicated and incoherent feelings about difficulty. Like I bitched about it being too easy and now that it's asking me to try I want to make it easier? 

But then even if I finished this fight, then it's back to the grindstone of the overworld map stuff so the reward for beating it doesn't feel like a reward. So I could autoactivity and skip it but then why am I doing it? For a reward of a story that I just think is *okay*? 

I don't totally know why I feel this way and I do think I'm overly harsh and irritated at a game that I do think is actually pretty good. If someone asked me about this game I'd say it's both very disappointing and it bums me out and it's also a pretty good game and a fun time. It's like riding the line of C+/B- for me right now but my feelings are so much more negative than that feels. 

I do think part three can be fun. I can see a world where a bit more rested I might have a bit more patience for the piles and piles of mechanics and the difficulty and be able to forgive the plot and go along for the ride. So a little pause is probably good.
