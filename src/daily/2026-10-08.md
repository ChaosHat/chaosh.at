## fefw
There is so much shit in part 3 and it's causing my mind to implode and also explode simultaneously. Too many mechanics. Part 1 was like "this is a little much" and part 2 was "where did all the things to do go?" and part 3 is "here they are also they found friends and also have fun managing armies."

Prime brain melters:
- I can't fit all the people I like on teams 
- Trying to balance multiple teams 
- Who do I even promote to what and then I have to get more classes still? 
- Trying to do literally everyone's overworld mechanic at once. 
- New overworld mechanics like rebuilding things.
- Probably some other things.

There are also just things I wish I had known in advance which I feel like is the achilles heel of this game. I stopped gathering resources because I had stopped needing them. SORRY YOU NEED THEM. For how long this game is I think it's really unfortunate how often it makes you wish you had known something tens of hours ago. 

Maybe that's the point. It's about time travel and changing the past. Now I know how they feel.

Also the unit merging mechanic is pretty overblown. Changing classes isn't going to really move the RNG growths needle very much and even when it does it's just as likely to be in a stat that character doesn't care about.
