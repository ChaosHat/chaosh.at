## smrpg
I beat the spear boss, Yaridovich, and that took *forever*.  I was mostly just having Peach heal tank it, she barely escaped his aoe water attack with 3 hit points left over and also got one shot with his stab attack. We took so long to fight him that he ran out of FP. This is the Homer Simpson boxing strategy. 

I think I'm done with this one too. It's just so *samey*. And I had this awful fight against the Piranha Plant boss, I wiped three times for some pretty terrible feeling reasons, this game *loves* status effects that take away control of your character. I tried to have Peach use the anti-mushroom effect accessory and it just took some luck for her not to die and be able to take it down.

I'm not sure what it is with the perspective here but I found jumping between these to be really challenging! Too many gat dang minigames here. 

There's just not enough combat novelty and now that I have Peach I feel like I've kind of solved the game as an attrition battle. The moment I tried to NOT use Peach I ran into that Piranha plant and I desperately needed her heal and status cleanse in one. 

There's something to analyze here about why this isn't working for me. I don't feel like this game is fundamentally less complex than a lot of things I've completed recently like DQV. Maybe there's just an element of burnout here but I'm not so certain about that. It's no Thousand Year Door and that game is also *pretty thin*.
