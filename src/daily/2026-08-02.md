## Dragon Quest XI S
This game is pretty boring and basic and yet I still am interested in it after like eight hours? No idea what it is. I think it's short enough with just enough visible over the metaphorical narrative hill and minimal friction to get there to be like "okay let's go see what's over there." I have no idea if it'll pull me through the entire runtime, it's very long, but I said that about P5R.

## Final Fantasy VII Remake
This game feels so...welcoming and cozy at times. The sector 7 slums section really just feels like hanging out with friends getting to know each other after a long time which, it is. I also think Tifa's theme feels so warm.
