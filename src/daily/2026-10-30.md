## Burnout 3: Takedown
I turned on widescreen and in the menus this feels stretched but in races it seems...fine? I'm not sure, but it does seem easier to read wider. The rubberbanding in this game feels pretty unfair sometimes, very one sided. The CPU is always right on your ass but then you'll be an insane amount of seconds behind...and growing. It's especially tough on the eliminator races where there just aren't other opponents to hit to get the boost to get back into it. The tension is good, it pushes you to take those risks, but it can feel a little all or nothing. 
## Nintendo Switch Sports Resort
Played some of this with Lillian this afternoon as she had an early dismissal. Did a smattering of sports/events, we tried the archery variant where everyone hits the same target, that was pretty fun. You have to determine how much you want to risk a good shot and maybe giving your opponent points, or just playing it safe. 

Also tried the three point basketball challenge and had a buzzer beating winner which was fun. I don't totally get table tennis, I don't understand what translates into a good hit or where it goes, and regular tennis seems like it's on a very weird delay. We also did bowling which is classic and I set a new personal high. 

The jump rope warm up is not joking around either.

## Aurora Watch
Another thing I've tinkered with a *bunch* lately is images! I wanted to start documenting my playtime with screenshots for a few reasons. I think it's fun and is a nice way to kind of remember what I was doing. I think it helps break up the text some so I'm not just writing tomes and tomes of things. And it's just more colorful!

The first version was one where it just spanned the entire text column, which wasn't great because that felt like too hard a stop for me. I don't want the image to just completely break up the flow of the reading (sometimes that can be a good tool, it's not what I want to be the default). 

The next one I tried looked the best by far, it was a more magazine style layout where it half indented the text which flowed around it. I really, really liked how this looked but it was so annoying to format. The text would wrap weird and I would spend a bunch of time trying to move the pictures around, modify my sentences and paragraphs to hit length targets for the pictures. That was work that is maybe viable for one off essay work, but is annoying to have to consider for dailies which are supposed to be easy and *mostly* stream of thought. I do some light editing and organization of thought before they go out but that's about it. 

So this is my middle ground, the right side gutter for images. If anyone reads this and has feedback please let me know! Also, if anyone has feedback on where they'd prefer an Aurora Watch to be! I put a few at the top, but then I considered any reader might want to know more about the games first, so now it's at the end.
