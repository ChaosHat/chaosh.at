## cbc
Alright, we unlocked the crazy mirror world! Huzzah. I do dislike how it won't let you use the dang map so I can get my bearings. You jerks. The amount of hoofing it to get the unlocks for this was okay but sub ideal. The arrangement of this map is a little worse than one big castle, there are a lot more linear stretches, you can't just make a loop and hit everything, you have to backtrack a lot as part of your back tracking.

![[20261019225038_1.jpg]]

*She really does suck, doesn't she Garth?* 

This mirror world is a pain in the ass though. I just had to break down and look at a guide. Without the map it's just too challenging to keep my heading and figure out what I have left. Nothing in the area itself was too hard, it was just keeping it straight. 

We also beat ourselves a Succubus boss. This was the easiest one, partially because I think the whip absolutely broke it. She just floated in mid air and let me absolutely wail on her. It's disappointing because the whip is the most thematic weapon but it feels too good. 

Thankfully, this game gives me a real heading now with the queen having absconded by herself. I expect that to quickly resolve itself and leave me wondering where to go next though.

Final Fantasy Resonance reviews should drop tomorrow morning. Real excited for that. A late entrant to the GOTY possibilities and then we're on to some backlogging. Things I want to try before year end (besides Resonance) are Monster Hunter Stories 3, Galactic Racer, and FFVII Rebirth.
