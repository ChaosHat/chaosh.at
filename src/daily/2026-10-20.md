I haven't talked about the spells too much. Some thoughts about spells:
- The Gargoyle has to be the worst spell. It's so useless and the Works of Mercy for it seem tough. Going to have to grind that out. 
- I really like Mammon's spinny disk, it's a fun mini game to manage during combat. 
- The succubus life drain is okay. Having to just stand there feels sub optimal, it's not enough damage to overcome that.
- The blood tide that causes bleeding damage is phenomenal. One of my favorites. I wish it worked on bosses.
