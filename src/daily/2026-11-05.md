## Final Fantasy Resonance
![[3259780_20261105134106_1.png]]

*Slightly overkill on this trash mob.*

Alright, I went back to challenge Sephiroth and equipped everyone with the proper visions and built out the load out and...got my butt handed back to me for 45ish minutes? He's tough. The party is around level 48 and it's something I think I *could* beat but would have to get a little lucky to do so. So I'll just level some more and come back. 

One thing that's very annoying is the lack of loadout settings for the characters. It's tedious to have to go in, move everyone's visions and abilities around for the fight, then finish and then set it all back. That's probably one of the bigger reasons I haven't wanted to do a higher difficulty level is that it would require a lot of living in menus like that. 

![[3259780_20261105104226_1.png]]

*I should probably use more Sakura.*

I think I might need to be more mindful about pushing this thing to the end a bit more too. I can kind of feel that listless feeling in my brain, this'll usually happen when it feels like I've kind of seen everything I need to but not everything I want to, or my brain thinks I want. A lot of times I'll be enjoying the game just fine but I'll get bogged down in optional content so I can see it all and keep enjoying the game for longer! So I want to beat all the optional bosses and do all the optional things because I should shouldn't I?

![[3259780_20261105153421_1.png]]

*Occasionally silly.*

The reality is that I almost never care. I thought about continuing Belmont's Curse to 100% it after I was done. I have had zero actual desire to do that. I got what I wanted and with a couple days distance after beating it, I don't feel like I'm actually missing anything of value. 

After going back and advancing the plot nearly to the end of chapter 7, I hit around level 51 and went back to try Bahamut and Sephiroth, taking both down. The Bahamut fight was pretty straightforward, no issues. 

![[3259780_20261105153644_1.png]]

*Goodbye, One-Winged Angel*

Sephiroth still took a couple tries, it's a somewhat annoying fight because it really relied on me getting the right hit with Lid's Mechamog Scan. Once I did though, he went down pretty easily with a couple hairy moments. We mostly got there by abusing the crap out of action multipliers, Rain just going to town with with Nichol's tier two limit break doubling all his doublers. 

I do think the Sephiroth fight taught me I can be a little more flexible in my party comps. I've basically had Lasswell, Fina, and Nichol stapled to Rain (this party also feels mostly canonically correct, the Nichol slot is maybe the most flexible one) but I got through Sephiroth by being able to use some healing visions. 

![[3259780_20261105220614_1.png]]

*Lid you dummy, never say this. What a jinx.*

Afterwards I wrapped up chapter seven's epic conclusion. I didn't expect a vision of Raegan. I kind of don't know what they are? They all seem bound to my characters, it's not like they do anything or have agency. That would be cool though, if you could talk to Cloud or Terra and they would give advice or insight at certain plot moments. 

The bad guys that replace the Sworn Six seem a lot more generic and less interesting which is a bummer way to go. The Sworn Six at least had an interesting motivation beyond "rar my brain is broken kill all life."

![[3259780_20261105223552_1.png]]

*Now it's my One-Winged Angel*

Finished up Rain's quest and I'll probably do some grinding out of their special weapons at the battle tower or whatever. I did the first fight there to unlock Rain's special weapon. I got to unleash Sephiroth on this poor bastard.
