This game rocks. While it doesn't do anything particularly new or groundbreaking, I love how it looks, the music is great, and it's a very chill and comfy experience. I really like it a lot so far. We are truly spoiled for great games right now. I think this game is going to fly real under the radar and that's a bummer because I would really like more. 

Took down a bunch of bosses, some optional, got a new esper, and beat a little girl at jump roping. The bosses have a recommended level but I think that number is too conservative. They were never really too big a challenge and I usually came in at around or a level under that recommendation. I never felt like I needed to pick my visions based on attacking their weaknesses. We downed a water dragon, a rukh, and a shoggoth. 

Got Nichol on the team and I'm really interested to try him out further. Lid was an interesting character but in combat she just felt okay. I think Nichol's support leanings will make him super useful in any fight.
