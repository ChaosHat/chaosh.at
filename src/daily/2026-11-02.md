## Final Fantasy Resonance
![[3259780_20261102130559_1.webp]]

*Max power Mechabo!*

So I did the Rain and Lasswell duel which was, off the top of my head, the best scene in the entire game. Although it was a little weird. They have this Goku/Vegeta thing going on where they each want to push each other to the edge, but they're somewhat afraid of the consequences of what would happen if Lasswell wins? It has this exchange that was really funny. 

> **Rain:** You're going easy on me Lasswell! Why would you do this?  
> **Lasswell:** I'm afraid of what it would do to you if I beat you!  
> **Rain:** Do you think so little of me? If you beat me I'd be happy for you and I'd train until I beat you. Our friendship can withstand this.  
> **Lasswell:** Okay, here goes!  
> *Lasswell handily defeats Rain.*  
> **Rain:** Oh no he beat me! Not like this Lasswell, everything is ruined!  
> **Lasswell:** Oh no I beat him, everything is ruined! Why did I fly too close to the sun? All I ever wanted was to be assistant to the regional manager! I'm not regional manager material!  
> **Rain:** Why Lasswell? How could you have done this to me? To us? Our friendship cannot withstand this!  
> *Rain spaces out thinking about his glowing inner light*  
> **Rain:** Oops actually I do have more power, let's go again!  
> **Lasswell:** Okay!  
> *Rain beats Lasswell*  
> **Both:** Hooray! The proper balance is restored!

There is high drama here but they're both being a little ridiculous. Lasswell's motivations also seem a bit unevenly characterized. There were times when I thought he might flip out of jealousy, a more true Vegeta character who burns with a secret hatred of someone who gains something so effortlessly that they struggled with. His inner Salieri. On the other hand, he was so loyal and good the rest of the time it felt hard to believe he could ever be torn about his adoptive brother.

## Nintendo Switch Sports Resort
Played some with Lily again today. We tried volleyball and I didn't like that much at all. The timing did not feel intuitive. Both tennis games are fine, table tennis is maybe a little better. Bowling and archery are both great, although I did not like 100 pin bowling at all. It's annoying that there's no option for simultaneous play on it. It overall just feels very gimmicky and there's less ability to recover on a second throw.
