## fefw
Part 2 has definitely been a step up in challenge, really starting with chapter 3. It has just waves of enemies, reinforcements, and hostile terrain that makes fighting through it really a slog. But a good slog. It has made them less easy breezy bouts that I feel like I can just do while half paying attention. I gotta focus up. Which is a good thing overall, although I think it does mean I'm kind of ripe for a second game to be a little more chill. 

I really like how sand is a tool you have to monitor and be careful with again. It got to the point where I was throwing caution to the wind because maps were easy enough, or just preemptively spamming Aurora's blessing to bulldoze the maps. They're now long enough where I can't clear them in ten turns and I have to be really tactical about it. 

Additionally, the cost of Fortuna's blessing is so high, I used to just rewind, activate Aurora's blessing, and then pass the turn as is. Now that sucks. If I knew I needed to Aurora in advance, that would have saved me five sand! Five! Kalla's blessing feels like a luxury again, but tactically so necessary sometimes. 

Finished through part 2 chapter 5 now. That was a fight. At multiple points I was angry at the developers, those cheeky bastards. I think it took two hours. There were times where I had to go look and consider the stars of my units to choose who would be best to tank a hit. 

I discovered some of the optional skits I missed before on YouTube. Some of these are so plot relevant it absolutely boggles my mind that they're missable cutscenes and little battles. They're fully voiced! 

This actively bothers me and kind of lowers my grade of this game. Why would you do this? How is it acceptable? The only way I would have seen all of these is to have had a second screen open for it. 

I'm watching them now on Youtube, out of order because the uploader didn't put them in a specific order so I'm not getting the context in the larger story and this feels like meeting them MORE than halfway.
