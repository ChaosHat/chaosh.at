## Aurora Watch
Alright, some more playing with the nav and layout. I’ve recently been getting into some more indie web stuff, just looking around at the community and what's out there. One cool tool I found was bubbles.town. When I looked around I found I wanted to read more of what other people are doing and be a part of it. And not just a part of it, but in conversation with people. I want to read their stuff and write back to talk about things with people I like in a slower, more long form format. So I applied to some webrings. I got this absolutely lovely comment on my pull request to be added:

![[Pasted image 20261103224315.png]]

It feels so good to be seen and to have some acknowledgement from someone that the thing you're doing is cool is...well it helped make my night. If you want to make someone feel good, take time to comment on their blog. I've got a guestbook right here if you want to! And it's something I want to do more of for other people. It's a very easy thing to do to make someone happy. 

One thing I determined is that I wanted this to be a more general, slice of life space. That required changing how things work. The issue was that I had intended for this to be pretty scoped to media, games, shows, movies, books probably, but I wanted to be able to write about anything now. So, that ruined my plans for how the nav was structured. I want to be able to talk about sports, dabbling in photography, politics, whatever it is I'm interested in. I'm hoping if I'm interested, you'll be too. What's that saying? "Interested people are interesting people?"

At the same time, I want it to be easy to be able to zoom in on the stuff you're actually interested in. Each topic can be reduced to its own view on the site, so if you only want to see me ramble about video games or even a specific one, you can definitely do that. 

I'm getting rid of the submenu that shows up on the main page, it'll only show up on the specific pages for that type as opposed to just having the submenu always open. The new submenu is styled as a pop up box that feels in line with the old turn based RPG menus it's inspired by. Each tab or group is clickable and links to a splash page for a feed of just that topic's posts. 

There's a tab for etc, which is where more topics can live as a catchall. The main menu is going to cap at six total tabs (so three topics plus etc, essays, and about), with extra topics potentially getting promoted/demoted out of that six as there's action, with the overflow living in etc. There isn't a specific function that automates that promotion/demotion, it's just something that's going to have me keep an eye on it. 

We've reduced the distance from the top of the page to where the words I write actually start by...a lot! I think it was too many boxes and menus and now we've tightened it and moved the old slogan to a footer. I also removed the latest essays part and made essays just a part of the menu...we'll see how much I like that. I kind of like highlighting the stuff I put the most effort into but it makes logical sense to me and again, saves space!
