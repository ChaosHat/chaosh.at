EA shuttered BioWare and while the studio has been effectively dead for a while, it's still shocking to see them officially throw in the towel on what has been an amazingly influential studio. BioWare games (along with Blizzard) really defined a lot of my growing up, if you check the game shelf Mass Effect, the original two Baldur's Gates, and KOTOR all rate highly for me still. 

If there's a silver lining, it's that in a few years we'll get a bunch of games "from the makers of Mass Effect" in new studios. Best of luck to everyone out of a job though, I know exactly what that's like. 
## cbc

![[20261021224132_1.jpg]]

*Spooky.*

I finished 100%ing Notre Dame! Since the game is kind about letting you finish 100%ing it after you beat it I decided to finish it. I'll see how I feel about going for the full clear, but it's nice to just get it off my plate with a clear conscience before Resonance. 

This is also something where I'll probably feel better about just idly doing this on the Thor or something. I had expected this to feel like something I'd be able to carry as my second quick and light game but it's been fun enough to occupy my whole attention.

The Dracula fight is challenging, my biggest complaint is that the mid fight cutscene is unskippable. It is appropriately epic though. This is by far and away the best Castlevania in my opinion. It's much more Metroid-like than the others. I'll probably have more decompression thoughts but it's late and I do need to go to bed.

## lazarus

![[Screenshot_20261021-215232 1.png]]

*Meet the Goomster.*

Third gym is down! We've made some team adjustments mostly because I got tired of this dumb dragon and got a cute one instead. Goomy is a cool dude and I want to find a metal coat to make it the Hisuian flavor. 

The game does feel a little grindy, I keep having to go back to heal and stuff every few fights. That's one of my biggest complaints with Pokemon, I don't think they give you enough stuff to actually feel self sufficient while exploring. A lot of other JRPGs give you free innate healing at the cost of MP, but healing seems so expensive in Pokemon. You really have little recourse but to go back to town, especially with a faint in the early game. That feels so painful with limited mobility on longer treks.
