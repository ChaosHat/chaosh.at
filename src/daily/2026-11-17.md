## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
![[2026-11-17 12.11.09.webp]]

*This is a beautiful hallway, Ganon has taste.*

So, we get to Ganon's castle. It's all come to this. First of all Ganon, some bold choices here. Also, what do you even DO here? Just here in your floating castle in the middle of nowhere with your monsters doing...something.

The trial rooms here are a fun culmination of every tool you've gained and the puzzle design from the earlier parts. It also combines things, requiring multiple elements at once to solve the puzzle instead of the standard dungeon design where you only use the thing you just got. This is the clearest counterexample of my issue with A Link Between Worlds's progression and endgame and what I felt was lacking there. 

![[2026-11-17 12.11.31.webp]]

*A real flair for the dramatic.*

I suppose the downside of the endgame being that culmination of everything is that this section really emphasizes the issues with the control scheme. You have to change items a LOT. I found it annoying and I even had the benefit of being able to assign extra things to the dpad. 

The fact that the fire barrier requires the golden gauntlets seems somewhat annoying since you could theoretically get to this room before acquiring them unless I missed something. Are they just assuming you'll dispel the shadow one first and therefore have the golden gauntlets? The fakeout ending at the end of the light section also makes me wonder how many people thought their game was bugged and quit. 

![[Pasted image 20261117134318.webp]]

*In the M rated version Ganon is giving you the finger here too.*

I do respect how Ganon knows he's evil, is definitely not the protagonist in this story, and doesn't even care. Just own who you are. He also understands that he's a drama queen. He built this lair to have a long, dramatic stained glass stairwell so you could hear him playing his pipe organ. Yes, the entrance to the stairway is a gigantic fangorious mouth. Also the entrance hallway inside the barrier being full of fire keese is a real dick move. Ganon knows how to be evil too.

Why is there a boss key in this linear path to Ganon's lair? Like I already had to pass a much more difficult barrier and then why put the key here instead of some much more out of the way spot? I would love to talk to the developers on why they'd even include it here. "Well if we didn't give you a boss key and door then you wouldn't even know this Ganon fight is the boss."

![[2026-11-17 12.19.18.webp]]

*Zelda master of gate opening.*

The final boss fight sequence is pretty satisfying. The fight is appropriately epic and interesting. The first phase fireball bouncing is a cliche but it's always fun and a series staple. In the second phase I remembered blasting him with light arrows in the face to stun him but it took me a moment to remember what to do after I ran out of magic. It turns out you just roll under his legs and smack him in the tail.

And then after that, that's the end of the game! Overall I think it holds up and I really question the need for a full on remake, I feel like you could have slapped on a fresh coat of paint, call it a 4K remaster and it would have been good enough. I've heard people tout the jump button and other changes but I've never felt like I needed to jump all the time. 

![[2026-11-17 12.36.31.webp]]

*You can't send me back I know too much.*

Zelda is so weirdly portrayed in this game. Sheik seems capable and is a ninja and escapes in a flash, and then when you're escaping the tower she gets trapped in this fire thing and a couple of Stalfos come out and she can't do anything? But she also has magic powers? 

The ending of this is also weird as many people have noted. Is adult Link still mentally a child? And does child Link still have all the memories of adult Link? That's probably why Link doesn't talk. These would be thorny issues to have to come to the forefront. "Link you must find the other sages-" "WHAT IS HAPPENING TO MY BODY?!"
## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2026-11-17 14.21.09.webp]]

*I love the throwback look of these.*

Chadley is just like my least favorite coworker at every job. He's overly chipper about his work, he just talks all the time and goes on and on while I'm trying to do other things. He makes it hard and awkward to get away from him. He keeps trying to have meetings that could have been emails. He seems like the kind of person who would really abuse a reply all. 

Did basically all of the Junon stuff I can do before advancing the story further. There's a couple world intel things you can't get to until later. I forgot the protorelic here was playing Fort Condor. Now this is a minigame I like. There's definitely a clear difference in my appreciation for these based on the amount of effort put in. Fort Condor and Queen's Blood could just be standalone games. 

![[2026-11-17 22.04.18.webp]]

*Cloud you idiot you can never say this to your partner.*

Speaking of, after getting into Under Junon I played a couple more games of Queen's Blood before saving Yuffie's hide. Yuffie is dumb but I do find her charming for some reason. A lot of the cast is like that, they work better than they necessarily should for how tropey and silly they are. I think it's the absolute earnestness behind them. 

I also enjoyed the little party story scenes in the inn. A quick little bit with them where you try to figure out which answer will resonate with them best. Tifa's felt the most obviously impactful but I enjoy the interactions with most of the characters.

![[2026-11-17 22.09.42.webp]]

*Roche has extreme 90s himbo energy and I love it.*

Once the story stuff was done and I met Roche, yet again another character who should be too dumb and yet I do like them, I escorted Rhonda's dog to unlock the other town. I forgot about the totally insane bow wow wow song for the dog. Also the ridiculous Queen's Blood subplot.

Also, the piano is another fun minigame. I find it challenging and overwhelming in all the right ways and it's a pretty unique idea for a rhythm game. It's a bit of a spatial puzzle too it feels like, since you have to control two sticks in different directions.
