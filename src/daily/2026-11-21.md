## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261121-115927 2.webp]]

*Goodbye, Sector 7*

Wrapped up Wall Market, got all the parts for Cloud's outfit. I'm using the Shinra Archaeology mod so it's a little different but not outrageously so. It adds some extra scenes in the Honeybee Inn which are mostly just comical and weird. It gets you to the same result. 

Got through the sewers and you know, I'm not upset that it's so much shorter than the Remake version. Overall Remake feels like it comes in at a fairly reasonable playtime and I wouldn't fault it for excessive bloat, but this is the part of Remake that is overexpanded. The sewers and train graveyard run long.

![[Screenshot_20261121-194022.webp]]

*Tifa seems a lot more gassed on the stairs in the og.*

The difference in what feels overlong versus actually good expansions is what adds more to what's important here: the characters. The extra Turk fights make the Turks more interesting as characters and villains. Making the sewers longer to fight Abzu still is just...more of the same. The elements that make Jessie and the rest of Avalanche realer characters helps you care more when they die. 

Finished up midway through the Shinra tower. I took the stairs of course. It's amusing running into random mobs that got upgraded to boss status in the remake like the Hell House or Swordipede. 
## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Remake)
![[20261121165839-01M801548WHJ6A58V0N1RZ0F5E.webp]]

*I'd love a nice cool glass of Barinade.*

Finally got back to this. Funnily enough today, November 21st, is exactly the 28 year anniversary of the N64 release. I've been mostly waiting to play with my daughter so she could experience it. I really wanted her to at least watch everything through the twist so she wouldn't come back and be like "wait why is Link big?" You get most of the plot development that way, there's not a ton extra as an adult. 

Jabu Jabu was fine, no notes. We wrapped that up, did a few little extras, traded the Keaton Mask, grabbed a few Skulltulas, then yanked the sword from the dais. The new cutscenes are overall fine. 

![[20261121165852-01M8015GV5WZBYN9893GCCC9QS.webp]]

*Actually exactly 28 years Navi...*

So far I feel like such a thoroughly faithful remake is kind of a missed opportunity. It looks nice mostly and has a bunch of quality of life improvements but to me I'm not sure that feels like enough for me personally. I get that a lot of people aren't going to do Ship of Harkinian and mod and emulate it and whatever, but I am and this feels like a marginal improvement at best. 

I would have rather a more modern reimagining. Something that hits the same beats but expands on it, adding something new and interacting with the source material more. And I don't think you needed to do a full "what if we did this but it was Breath of the Wild" style. Do what an old school Zelda would be if we did it now. Or like, what is the FFVII Remake version of this?
