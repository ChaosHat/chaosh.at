Alright, I've beaten it now. Didn't mean to, just didn't realize there was literally no saving after you step through the glowy portal. 

I think this game is a rare jrpg that earned it's insane epicness. It makes sense to fight fate here, much more than it made sense for high schoolers to fight the god of control. It seems that by cashing the checks it wrote all those years ago. We understand why these characters have to fight fate and why here, this time, it's appropriate. 

I really like this game overall though. I'm not even someone who's that nostalgic for the original. I played it once in high school and thought it was fine! 

Probably won't roll directly into Rebirth after? Before I didn't think so for sure, but the ending is so epic it just makes you want to keep going. Might just do Jedi Fallen Order.
