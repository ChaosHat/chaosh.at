## Bears
This is a game we should win which always makes me more nervous than the tight competitive ones. I'm very used to the Bears not taking care of business. Caleb looked amazing through the first, I think even if he retired tomorrow he's the best quarterback in Bears history. And yes, he's thrown ~~two~~ ~~three~~ four picks tonight but he's also been amazing. 

It's also incredible how one new player on defense can turn me from dreading defense to being excited. Jeffrey Simmons has been a revelation. This defense is really just night and day since the grace. 

This has been rougher than I would have hoped but I'm not panicking yet. Surely this is a combination of Caleb/Ben being bored of leading continuously and needing everyone to see how worth it the Simmons trade was. 

Honestly, if we win this, when we win this (currently writing this line at 5:05 left in the 4th) this win will mean a lot. You're going to have terrible bad games where everything goes wrong but you want a team resilient enough to come through. You want the other phases to lift each other up when needed. 

We did it. We had enough when we needed it to get there. 
## Sovereign Tower
We made it to act three. It was pretty trivial to beat the Kingslayer even with only three of four knights available as I had all the requirements passed. I ended up tight on knights for a while. I opted to undo The Wolf's curse and got Rufus, but he was gone for the Kingslayer confrontation so I was sweating that a bit. Also, to pass the money goal I had to not pay Gwendan's debts which meant I lost him, but he's back! 

Other choices I made (probably useful to remember for future runs): I gave Arron the heart which I kind of regret. His new ability seems detrimental. Dulahan is the leader of Arnor now, having won the tournament, which is probably good for them. Having a demon knight on the throne and all. I got Childeric. Also I should note that Goberto was the one who died somewhere (although I think that's a given with Dulahan.)

At this point I've mostly decided to just press on. This game goes pretty quick and I'll probably replay it. I'd be interested to see a very straight no time travel run, and then possibly a lots of time travel run. There are definitely multiple quests/plotlines that require the use of time travel. 

This reminds me a lot of Esoteric Ebb. It's not quite as funny but it's in the same space. 

Continuing on, dang I'm really in a crunch for knights now. Ursula left because I romanced her and then I kissed the Lady of the Tower... Listen, she's a magic statue lady whose curse I could end with a kiss. How could I not?

I do think the Round Table eventually becomes a bit cumbersome. There are a bunch of knights (I don't even have the max!) and so many quests and items. It would be nice if there was a quick optimizer, and also some more difficulty instruction and calibration. I've come across some quests that allow two knights to go and I think I could do it with one.
