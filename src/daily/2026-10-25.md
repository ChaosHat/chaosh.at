## you can see everything
And boy, do I have something to talk about. This movie was so fascinating. I instantly kind of got the message of this movie. Holmes is so...captivating and somehow charming in her odd intensity and quirks. You feel compelled to believe her, but then there's so much evidence against her. It seems plausible that she could have been in the dark about some things, but not *everything.*

But then you see how she acts and her blind faith in her new terrible husband and you think "yeah, maybe this is what happened." She's willing to trust this guy (who absolutely sucks) with everything. No second guessing, no checks. Nothing. So maybe she's just naive and much too trusting. And taken advantage of. 

Then I start to consider how maybe that's me being sexist. Oh, this poor innocent white woman couldn't be the mastermind, she was taken advantage of by mean men. But maybe the other view, that she's a wicked siren who manipulated all these powerful men and wrapped them around her finger is the other sexist extreme!

The whole thing about getting paid for the doc is just more fuel for this ambiguity. Does she actually not care about the money? Or is she just manipulating, pulling the strings, and she knows this dude is exactly the kind of patsy, a fall guy, who could conceivably be seen as taking advantage of a woman who loves too much, too quickly. 

We'll likely never know the answer. That's what makes her such a compelling figure. You can see what you want in her and you can be right, the evidence exists to support any take you'd like. Whichever the answer actually is it's very clear that something is broken inside her. 

If she's a liar, she's the best to do it. She's exceeds George Costanza levels, I think even he would have had shame at some point. He would have broken down and given up. If she has a mask, it never slips. I think lesser liars would have slipped up in their need to be perceived as masters of their domain, competent, and smart. I'm not sure a liar that pursues what she attained would be okay with being seen as not understanding Who's On First. 

And if she's not a liar, she's just...too inherently trusting and something prevents her from just seeing through these people or understanding how she comes off or the ambiguity of what she says. 

I think I'll probably have more to say about it. It seems like the kind of thing I'll continue to chew on, which I think is the sign of a pretty good movie.
