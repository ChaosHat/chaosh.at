## fefw
Leda is definitely the most complicated character of the three I've done so far so I'll be interested to see how they pull it off. There's obviously a terrible darkness to her but she's also a very nice and sweet person, so I'm interested to see how they reconcile this. Will it be believable as a unified whole or will it feel disjointed?

Her allies also feel the most...loosely aligned? Basically everyone on Cai and Theodora's runs were very highly on board with the plan and the vision. Everyone seems to be supporting Leda if not so much her goals. This isn't a problem, in fact I think it kind of builds extra texture for them. 

Sirocco is the one I think I'm iffiest on, but I have a feeling we're going to learn something underlying that redeems him? He'll still suck though. Well and Buccar just seems...fine.
