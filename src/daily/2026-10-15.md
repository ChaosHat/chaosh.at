## cbc
Oh yeah, this game is fun! It took a little for me to get used to the controls, I still had muscle memory from Prince of Persia, but after that we're good to go.

We beat some bosses! I liked the second boss, ||Joan of Arc||, which was a bit of a challenge until I learned her patterns. Medusa wasn't too bad, although I killed her on a photo finish. After that I then found out that somehow, inexplicably, ||DRACULA|| was behind the whole thing. Who could have possibly guessed he was behind the shadows, plotting his return? Not me!

![[20261015222036_1.jpg]]

*Who could possibly be in this castle?*

I like the weapons in this although I do think there's an issue where you upgrade one and then it's just by far the best one and the other options seem much worse until you can find one. 

It's really fun though. I like the platforming, I'm figuring out the combat too. I think I just don't quite know how to read the things and time the parries yet but when you get it right sometimes it feels so good. It's so fun to use the whip and be able to zip around and slam into people. 

The difficulty, somehow, feels juuuuust right. Things are hard and we die but it never feels unfair. The platforming, the bosses, they seem attainable, I just need another couple tries and to tighten it up. I could also make a supercut of me just absolutely dying over and over again in some truly hilarious ways on some platforming challenges. 

![[20261015223243_1.jpg]]

*For an eternal vampire lord and avatar of Chaos he has some breathtaking decor.*

Sometimes this game looks absolutely *gorgeous.* This game does feed my ADHD in a specific way. There are so many directions to go! The music is an absolute bop, it was very cute when the library music got a lo fi beats version. 

The story is interesting for a Castlevania game, the extra "party members" that Rose gets through the tarot cards add some spice to a normally very solitary protagonist experience. 

Overall just having an absolute blast. Great game so far.
