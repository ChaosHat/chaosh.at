## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-24 06.51.16 1.jpg]]

*Hell yeah Ichiban.*

Of *course* this game has a sword in the stone bit with a baseball bat covered in barbed wire. I'm also angry at myself that I didn't realize the job system was...the job system. This game just makes me smile with this kind of stuff. 

I can't believe they made this soapland manager a good guy! The bonding scene in the bar was also so nice and heartwarming, seeing Adachi grudgingly admit that he likes the group and the bar workers outing him on that. This game is really about found families and it's really awesome to see these various people at the bottom of society band together to fight a system rigged against them. 

There are just so many moments that make you smile in this game. I loved how gung ho Ichiban was to take his vocational classes and the celebratory cutscene that plays when you pass is so over the top and so good. It's also absolutely insane, the sheep minigame when you're trying not to sleep in the movie theater is not at all what I expected.
