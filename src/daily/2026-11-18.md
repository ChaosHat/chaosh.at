So with the original Ocarina done, I have a slot open for a game. Usually I can juggle two games, something that's easy to run and playable in short stints on the Thor (or maybe the Steam Deck or Switch but Thor preferred), and a bigger game that wants or deserves to be played in longer sessions or looks pretty/is demanding enough. I've got a few things on my backlog to cycle through and we'll see what nabs me. 
## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2026-11-18 15.29.52.webp]]

*Beautiful Junon with its oversized anime sea gun.*

Continuing from my thoughts on the characters last night, Kyrie is the one character that I think doesn't actually win me over. She sucks so hard and I just roll my eyes every time I have to interact with her. I'm not totally sure about why, she's just obnoxious. 

I got through the remainder of the quests, did a few combat sims to unlock Phoenix and the Elemental materia before moving on to Junon proper. The final Fort Condor fight is so hard! I'm interested in trying the hard mode but not right now, that feels like it'd take a significant chunk of time.  

![[2026-11-18 11.10.16.webp]]

*My well-composed photograph of a well-composed condor photograph.*

The frog jumping minigame is also surprisingly fun for how simple it is. It's not the kind of thing you'd want to put 20 minutes into, but as a change of pace? Not bad. The dolphin one is a bit more ho hum. I get that they probably felt like they needed to do something for this, it's a prime opportunity. 

On the other hand, I'm not totally sure what the point is of little interactive bits like controlling the ship going up and down. It could have just been a cutscene. I'd love to know how these decisions are made. Who chooses which things need a little interactive quicktime event or minigame? "Damn it, we *need* a little segment where the players control the boat lift. How else will they feel immersed? This was a big part of the game trailer!"

![[2026-11-18 15.29.27.webp]]

*Hi guys, just working the boat lift.*

Rufus Shinra is such a turd and there are so many meticulous elements of his outfit that are absolutely insane. The absolute worst part is whatever he's doing with his belt, he's partially tucked it over one of his coats. Also his weird netting skirt, and the way he's buttoned and not buttoned his coat buttons in a way that says "I don't care" but you know he absolutely does and meticulously picked which ones to button and not? He's definitely a very hateable villain, this born on third base nepo baby. 

Parade prep ends up being such a fun little diversion. The girls are so cute in how excited they get to play dress up. The bald club is so funny, the music is marchy and patriotic, and the infantry units are so eager. Sure, these Shinra soldiers suck and I may have to stab them later but this also shows the insidious dark side of terrible organizations. They're built on a lot of normal people looking to pay the mortgage or swept up in the promises and propaganda. They're really excited to do this and show off and do their best.

![[2026-11-18 21.27.10.webp]]

*The Seventh Infantry: Midgar's finest and the pride of our people.*

It is weird to me that Cloud and the girls don't wear the goggles part of the uniform to go more incognito. Or that nobody notices Cloud's mako eyes? I thought that was a special thing of his specific Soldier First Class status. Or if it's not, wouldn't they notice Aerith and Tifa *don't* have that? PLOT HOLES.

My constant swapping of controllers from Switch/Thor Nintendo layout, Xbox style on the PC, and PS symbols really catches up to me in the rhythm mini game. Despite that (and having to restart a few times as a result), this is still a really fun sequence. The rhythm gameplay is good and I love how dynamically it's presented as a cutscene. Just as fun as it was in Remake.

![[2026-11-18 22.13.39.webp]]

*Dang it Yuffie.*

I feel bad for my troops. I hope they don't get in trouble for being hoodwinked by cloud. On the other hand they're pretty dumb and this is a giant security breach so maybe they do deserve it. But they were loyal and brave fighters who wanted to do their best and were proud about the honor their unit gained. They also knew Roche was a jackass which makes them okay in my book (but I do like Roche despite his jackassery).

I really hope I don't have to stab them later. That "will we see each other again, sir?" with Cloud's reply of "count on it" seems ominous. 

And that's chapter four, in the books!
## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261117-095039.webp]]

*Don't I know you? You look so familiar. Weirdest sense of deja vu.*

As I mentioned in a prior post, I do like the idea of playing both, there's an android recomp that lets you play with mods and I've never done that before. It's really interesting to play it and just see how different it both is and feels. Some of it is maybe just tone and nuance being lost in a text only format. Cloud feels a lot more snarky and uncaring. 

There's something very charming about the uprezzed ninostyle chibi characters. I love it when a game looks better in a way that's true to what it was, when it can make it look how it does through your rose colored nostalgia glasses. This is definitely that. 
## Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter
Alright, this runs acceptably well on the Thor for a trial run, although it's 30-40ish frames which is juuuuust playable. There are some other things about the game that might drive me a little crazy. 

The production here seems *real cheap* in some odd ways? Like the cutscenes don't voice everyone, just...one character? The fact that they're not voiced makes me feel like I'm going mildly crazy. It reminds me of that episode of Community where the characters are mouthing to each other and Abed goes "what's happening? Can you guys hear me? Am I deaf?" I wish that the cutscenes put the name in front of the subtitle since it's not immediately clear to me who's talking all the time since...they don't have voiced lines. 

And also the voice over doesn't even match the subtitle text. Why are they not working from the same script? It's not even close sometimes, like just one word here or there. Sometimes the entire sentence is constructed totally differently. For one example, the voiced line is "That was great!" and the text is "Thanks for the yummy breakfast!" At first I thought they were just canned barks being used to express the emotion but not the text, but some of it is too specific. Voiced: "You won't get to treat me like a kid anymore." Text: "Which means you'll have to treat me like the grown up I am!"

It does LOOK good so it's weird that these corners were cut. Although, I will say that as good as it looks, it doesn't feel like the performance should be this poor. I do feel like all of these things are kind of petty things to complain about a game with this much acclaim but with so many games out there to play this might just be enough to disqualify it. We'll see.
