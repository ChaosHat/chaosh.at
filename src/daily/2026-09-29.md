## fefw
Leda makes a pact with a demonic entity and then is surprised when the bill comes due. COME ON GIRL. 

This game has me thinking about why I play games. I find myself regretting that this game isn't harder to force me to reach deeper into the system and bag of tricks. Like I realized I never really use the second tier of Blaze Arts because well, I've never needed to. And how easy some of the Blessings can make the games (Aurora, Kalla). 

But I could choose to NOT do those things and make the game harder. But I dont' want to. And I don't know why. So do I want it to be harder? Or why do some kinds of harder feel more legitimate? I've never been a person for arbitrary self imposed challenge modes.
