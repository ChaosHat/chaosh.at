## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261122-083537.webp]]

*Uh this is not a good time with no materia.*

Oof, I got to the Rufus battle and lost because I couldn't reset my equipment. The commands were all showing the PC commands and not the controller keybinds so I started the fight without equipping anything. Annoying! Fortunately I can run the game back at 3X speed to get back to it. 

It's interesting to see the little things that are different. Like the fight Barret, Aerith, and Red get into in the elevator is a double boss battle because they moved the Airburster to the section where you scale back up to Midgar. Or they removed the part where you go to jail and you just nap in Aerith's room. 

![[Screenshot_20261122-150406.webp]]

*Would you believe me if I told you the next step was to fight Fate itself?*

The motorcycle chase is so janky and goes on too long. It's definitely more enjoyable in Remake. The camera angles are hard to read. I'm sure this absolutely blew some minds back in the day though. 

Materia and equipment management are definitely a little opaque here, keybinding issues aside. When the party splits up for Kalm the window to take materia off the other members is before you say to leave?

![[Screenshot_20261122-222002.webp]]

*And I helped!*

I managed to get to Kalm which is a little funny given I just started here what, a couple weeks ago? It's so interesting how faithful Rebirth's intro feels for a lot of these beats. As with everything else, there's definitely more in Rebirth than the original but Rebirth is really true to the tone of it. And as with the other, better uses of the expanded time, Rebirth really feels like it lets you get to know Sephiroth before everything went to shit. How he could have been a hero. 

If I don't stick with this, it'll be because the combat is just an absolute snoozefest in the original. But, portable, on the Thor with the ability to save at any time, it's been a real chill experience. 
## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Remake)
![[20261122143810-01M824CJNAXQWH6G4JH90X1PRC.webp]]

*Essentially the same as it was.*

It's shocking to me how poorly Epona feels to control still. This was actually one thing I was looking for to being better with modern controls and it still feels sluggish and finicky. This was really apparent at when trying to jump over the ranch fence. I don't know what it is but this has always been something that took me a couple tries and felt very picky about Epona's speed and angle. 

Speaking of controls, it boggles my mind how I have access to more buttons and the controls feel even more cramped and insufficient. I really wish I could have at least one ranged weapon plus something else always quickly available. I would trade the jump button back for an extra item button. 

![[20261122143800-01M827RCMZZQAFYWWZ6JXD481H.webp]]

*The Minuet of Forest is pretty underrated, it's a beautiful song.*

Grabbing the hookshot was more or less the same as it was in the OG. The Sun Song being able to switch times instead of always being the next dawn is definitely the biggest quality of life improvement, helps to grab some Skulltulas. No, scratch that. Zelda Notes is the biggest improvement although it does feel a bit like cheating. 

I did the first couple legs of the Biggoron's Sword quest and I suppose credit where credit is due, they do some amount of trying to expand on the prior lore here in little ways. I also noticed that in Kokiri village and the routing to the Forest Temple. They seemed to make the Kokiri care about you more, especially Mido. It does ring a little hollow for me because it always seemed to me that Link was considered kind of an oddity to be kept at arms length or worse by the Kokiri. Like everyone thought his no fairy status was contagious.
