## fefw
Act 2 really gives Theodora some main character vibes. Like we're all just living in her world. 

As I continue I think my main takeaway from this game is just there's not enough room for everyone. I really hope the next game is a bit more modest in scope. There are so many mechanics that feel like they received 60-80% of the time and attention they needed, so many plot threads and characters that feel the same. 

Like it's a game that's bursting at the seams and it's spilling out. Sometimes the greatest design note is knowing when to stop. I'm unconvinced this game has enough runway to land this. I know the length of the final two parts and I'm not sure it's going to satisfyingly wrap everything up. I remain skeptical that they're going to make me feel like Eshmel is/was a worthwhile inclusion. 

The part 2 is definitely closer to my personal desires for pacing but it's a bit bare. If part 1 is the overbloated part, part 2 really swings too far the other way. I would like some more tools to build my characters.

I know I've been hard on this game and it's mostly because I like it a lot. It just does a lot of things so well that it feels glaring where it messes up. And in some respects I don't want to beat them up too much, I want them to try and take swings! But in some ways I think they should have known better. Some of these things aren't fun, or they're obviously not as good as they should be. 

I also think I'm getting just a touch burnt out. I may try to carry a second game again for some variety. Star Wars Galactic Racer comes out tomorrow. That might be the ticket.
