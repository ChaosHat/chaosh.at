## The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Remake)
![[20261114165756-01M7E02FDBV73J8CZCF7D1R4FQ.webp]]

*We'll always be friends.*

Yeah I started this why not? I got it from my friend and decided to share with with my daughter. I got through Hyrule Castle, next stop Death Mountain. Well, actually I'll probably go to Lon Lon Ranch. 

The game does indeed look very good. The locations all really feel very...theme park-y to me. It has that heightened over realism that Disney World can have in its real immersive zones. That concept of having to really convey transporting you somewhere else in very little space. 

I like the voice acting but there's oddly not enough of it in weird ways. Like there's a voice acted scene with Zelda, then it goes back to being mute, and then it goes back to voice acted, then mute. With that, and also the extra dialogue, it really makes Link's mute thing seem weirder. 

They do a better job establishing Link as part of the Kokiri and Saria's friendship with him. Oh and Kaepora Gaebora is significantly less annoying. 
## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
Watched this tonight with my daughters and was really reminded of how fantastic this movie is. Spider-Man is my favorite superhero by far (I know, I'm basic) and this movie is a tremendous example of why. Spider-Man is so relatable. He's the everyman who has a higher calling. His life is terrible and yet he always gets back up. 

There's not a ton for me to say about it here that hasn't been said elsewhere. It looks great, the music is great, the performances are great. I just love this movie.
