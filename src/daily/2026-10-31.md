## Final Fantasy Resonance
![[Screenshot_20261031-094623.webp]]

*This game has some good looking scenes.*

We're back at it, making it to the final(?) island. Or are they continents? Anywho, immediately upon getting there, I immediately abandoned the main plot to do a bunch of side quests. The side quests are mostly pretty sweet, especially the party ones. I was a little surprised the follower quests don't have any combat, but that's made them a bit of a nice diversion. 

![[Screenshot_20261031-092242.webp]]

*Like the ears didn't tip that.*

I do wonder where they're going with this elf stuff. It's a curious amount of time to spend on something that appears to have no plot relevance so far. 

We've progressed to the doorstep of the final shrine and the plot beats just keep coming. I'm really liking it now. I'm going to guess, due to the flower in her hair, that non-Archwitch Fina is ||Rain's mom? There's probably some weird magical nonsense about Fina inhabiting her body or something as the soul left. ||
