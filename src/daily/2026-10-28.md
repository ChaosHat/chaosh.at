## Final Fantasy Resonance
Alright, had a couple tough fights where I had to either change a vision or alter the party order (because *someone* kept snorting them away). Nice to have a couple fun challenges. I've really considered upping the difficulty because the game feels a little too easy to force me to really interact with the systems, but I'm afraid it'll just make it feel grindier and more annoying. Optional T bosses (Titan and Typhon) down though. I also found where Sephiroth is and got promptly annihilated by him. 

![[3259780_20261028134201_1.png]]

*This did not go well, as you can see.*

It's absolute nonsense that I have to spend the hundred gil to rest on my own ship! Hey person running the inn, how about you let me sleep for free or I toss you overboard? This, like the tents, is something that just doesn't need to cost money at all and it's absolutely not a problem that it does, it just feels...insulting. 

In other minor bugaboos, this game has also trained me to not really bother buying the upgraded weapons and armor at shops. Too many times I've done that just to immediately find either that exact item or a better one for free in a random chest or something. 

![[3259780_20261028134339_1.png]]

*Mysidia! (It's only a model)*

I found Mysidia and it's the coolest (haha) place so far. I also got Sakura and let me just say, I really dislike the "looks like a child but is actually super old" trope that seems essentially obligatory in so many JRPGs. While on Mysidia, I gathered a bunch more visions, Lighting, Vaan, Victoria. Victoria's quest was amusing and I do like her. Love her design. 

It's so crazy how many visions there actually are in this game, it's such a love letter to this whole gigantic series. It makes me want to play more of them, although I've very recently bounced off a bunch of the old ones. The airship also allowed me to find a bunch of optional bosses and there are a BUNCH. And the level requirements are quite high. 

![[3259780_20261028142936_1.png]]

*Yet another vision or side character I'd rather recruit.*

Another crystal destroyed on our watch. We're doing a real bad job at crystal saving. Archwitch Fina is back and fortunately I was already leveling Fina on Y'shtola, so she can still heal. I do wish this game made it easier to swap party members in and out but there's very little reason to and I feel like I'm rocking the best crew (Rain, Lasswell, Nichol, Fina). The vision system definitely helps mitigate that thing I dislike in JRPGs where they take away your characters when you need them since the vision can help someone else fill in that job. 
## Burnout 3: Takedown
Saw a RetroGameCorps video on a new Xbox emulator and wanted to figure out something to test on it and it was this. This game immediately gave me so much nostalgia. The soundtrack, the destroying of things. It's a little hard to see things on the Thor screen which is a bummer, but man this is such a fun arcade racer to dip in, play a couple races in, and get out.
