## cbc
Yeah this game rocks. It's a blast. Spent a lot of time going around in giant circles, having fun exploring, but not really progressing anything. Then I got a good clue and some things clicked into place and I had some wind in my sails. Beat another boss. Let's go! 

![[20261016223759_1 1.jpg]]

*My heart is yearning, but Paris is burning.*

Thought I had a new heading. Nope. Did some back tracking with my new power. Got some cool upgrades! Found some level 3 weapons before getting the level 2 ones. 

So I guess what I'm saying is that this could use a bit better sign posting. But it's really fun. Again, the challenge level seems good and when it's clicking it clicks so good. Every time I run into the main hall and that theme plays it's just so excellent. 

I think I also figured out how you can get the "Beat the Game in 4 Hours" achievement. I think I found where to go to end the game but as it stands right now I would definitely get a bad ending. 

Nevermind, it was a bad ending. But hey, you get some extra cool scenes and an achievement and a skin! So how bad can that be?

![[20261016220612_1.jpg]]

*Let me hit the switch from long range with the spear you cowards.*

I don't have a specific weapon preference yet, they're all fun in their own way, which is a change from when I unlocked the initial set. I even liked the greatsword and I'm not usually a big slow weapon person. When I got the level 2 spear I liked it a lot, but I got the level 3 gloves soon after and found them to be strong. I bounced off the level one version (it felt like the uppercut always whiffed high) but this time it was clicking. Then I got the level 3 dual swords, the level 1s felt like t-rex arms, but now? Slice and dice. Maybe I just understand combat better. 

It still feels like a miss that you just get the upgrades you get and that they're so much better than the previous tier. It just forces you to use it. But maybe that's good because I wouldn't have come back to the fists. 

As for spells, petrify is a standout. The fact that it refunds its own mana is so useful. It doesn't work on bosses but it's a great tool for just moving around and fighting generic mobs. Nothing else has really stood out to me, the crucifix is kind of the de facto boss option. 
## lazarus
Pokedaisy is actually just really close to the optimal Pokemon playing experience. I just love knowing what attacks are good, being able to check my box or what Pokemon are in the zone and if I have them all. One thing I've really been prioritizing is not having to access a guide or a second screen and this does that. I can just stay focused on the game.
