## Nintendo Switch Sports Resort
![[Elgato Studio (2026-10-29 11-01-49).png]]

*Yes, that is I, Dad Parent Mike the Jellyfishman. Also: Nice.*

Did some more of this today, taking on the island champ series in archery and table tennis. Archery definitely loses some of its zen like quality when you're playing against someone. It obviously becomes much less about perfecting yourself and doing the best you can. Although, I suppose it still is and maybe that says something about how I view competition. I got to rank 10 in archery, the last one was tight and the first time I ever lost a set. I fared much better in table tennis, getting a rank jump to 13 after sweeping the first two or three opponents. I'm not sure which. 

This game is a nice little break to stand up and get active and I want to try and do it more regularly in little bursts for that reason. It is relaxing. Also you get 1000 coins to get stuff from the store the first time you play a game every day and I got this cool jellyfish head.
