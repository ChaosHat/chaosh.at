## Final Fantasy Resonance
![[3259780_20261101144251_1.png]]

*I don't need no staggers to beat this zombie.*

Went through the shrine dungeon some and then had about 10-15 minutes wiped out by a gamenative crash on the Thor. That's the first time that's happened, it's been pretty rock solid, but it was annoying. Got back into it and got to the next save point quickly, I was at least helped by knowing where the missable items were so I was able to minimize backtracking. 

Fought some tonberries, that's always a bit of a frightening moment when you encounter them in a Final Fantasy. We got to the end of this shrine and we got a "your crystal is in another shrine" moment. This has to be the gaming equivalent of "it was all a dream." 

![[3259780_20261101144344_1.png]]

*A mysterious last minute intervention?!*

I have hit what is essentially the final form here on some of these characters. Rain and Lasswell are monsters, multiattacking so hard. I am actually really liking this second shadow shrine, which is essentially a boss rush mode. This is something where I do kind of wish that I actually kept the difficulty higher because I'm able to just brute force a lot of these bosses and they're probably more interesting puzzles and challenges if you can't.

![[3259780_20261101154752_1.png]]

*The whole gang after slaying a series of bosses.*

This is something I always have trouble understanding about myself and is likely worth some additional self examination. Not that it's wrong, just to understand why. I could make this game harder and it would be more rewarding if I did, at least in certain aspects. But I don't really want to. Part of me doesn't want to just get bogged down and keep progressing. I think it might be some element of getting used to that pace and then feeling like I'm running behind. 

I think there's another frictional piece to it. So one, I don't want to have the difficulty increase on random mobs. That seems much too grindy for me. But I also don't want to have to spend more time in the menus swapping my stuff around, my visions, my party members. If the game allowed you to switch visions or tag in party members mid match, I think I'd be much more amenable to that. But having to quit, reload the save, then make your adjustments is quite annoying. Well, and there's some annoyance in having to rearrange everyone's loadout for one fight, then set it all back afterwards. So they could help me out and let me save a default loadout to restore back to. I suppose I could avoid that by just using a guide to know what I'm supposed to have for a load out in advance but I don't feel I should have to do that.
