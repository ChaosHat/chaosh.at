## cbc
This thing is on my goty shortlist for sure. Had a non fight with the artist, then I finished up the Library and Carmilla. Carmilla was the easiest fight yet, I got her in one shot and didn't even have to burn any potions. I really like her spell a lot. 

![[20261017162936_1.jpg]]

*About as close as it gets.*

Then, paintbrush in hand, we went to Notre Dame and got to beat up Death. Well, he beat me up a lot first. Death was probably the most challenging fight I've encountered so far. I died a *lot* which seems appropriate. It's funny how sometimes I would arrive to the fight with an extra health potion and think surely this is what will tip me over the edge. I got it this time. And then I'd immediately end up burning it on some stupid mistake. When I got him, it was by the skin of my teeth. 

The double jump and dash are definite back tracking game changes but unlike the ground pound it's not as immediately obvious where I need to go back and pick up some secrets. I might save backtracking again for when I feel like I have most of the powers so I'm not having to make the rounds nearly as often.
