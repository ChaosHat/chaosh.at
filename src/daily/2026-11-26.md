## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-26 22.36.36.webp]]

*When all hell is about to break loose.*

I think I've finished most if not all the sidequests available to me in chapter five. The Kuroi one was touching, it really hits on what must be a primal fear for people in that situation. Everyone has needs, to feel loved and loveable. I started to get very angry at that kid for throwing out the bookshelf. Thankfully that wasn't what happened. 

Also I sold some gold plates at the pawn shop. I had four so now I'm kind of loaded. I decked everyone out as best I could and then took a couple exams to boost stats. We're also taking the cab more which makes me wish I had at least talked to more cabs to unlock more fast travel points even when I wasn't using them before. 

![[2026-11-26 22.46.50.webp]]

*Looking dire, but also real fit.*

Oh my god, there's a whole business sim minigame. That's unfair. You can't give me that amount of one more turn addictiveness. I only stopped because the NPC for it left so we could go to dinner. I assume that's by design, to get me to play the regular game again. Which is what happened! 

I closed out chapter five and met a mysterious benefactor. It's interesting how a lot of the deus ex machina hasn't felt like that. It's just Ichiban's karma balancing out. He deserves it. 

This game continues to exceed expectations. 
## Bears
What an incredible game. I'm visiting family in Chicago for the holiday and the room was tense and then exploded right at the end. It was an awesome experience with the whole family in the room (aside from one person who is the ultimate negative, toxic doomer Bears fan).

I know this is easy to say after the fact but I never doubted. This team has been so resilient in the past (we haven't needed fourth quarter heroic comebacks in a while) and the Lions never fully put us away. We stayed within touching distance. The whole house was expecting a field goal at the end. I knew the whole time, we're getting a touchdown. 

The only thing I thought was kind of suspect was a bit of the clock management, I thought we wasted a bit too much time off the clock to give us enough shots at the end zone but it worked out. 

Insanely bummed about Colston Loveland but man, Roush really stepped up. 
## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261126-100518.webp]]

*Again, who play tests these things and thinks it's fun?*

Pressed on to Junon area, fought the boss and revived Priscilla. What is the deal with the CPR mechanic? I suppose this is evidence that random minigames that are essentially pointless and add nothing to the experience are baked deep into the DNA of Final Fantasy. The remake trilogy is doing right by the series. 

The boss fight itself was interesting. I didn't realize I could break the bubbles at first. Well I did try but it seemed like Cloud just hurt them. I ended up letting Tifa die and then Phoenix Downed her back up. Then when Aerith was bubbled Tifa cast an All Blizzard which hit the bubble and saved her. Huzzah! I was afraid it was a race and I was going to lose it.
