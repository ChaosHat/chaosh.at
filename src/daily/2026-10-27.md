Alright, I got a good grip of visions and I even got the next level of Magicite to be able to get some of them to level TWO. I made it through the rebel base and cleaned up most of the stuff to the weapons factory.

![[20261027153441_1.jpg]]

*Bahamut, not messing around*

I don't like fights that you're supposed to lose/cannot win. I think they're a waste of time. Just make it a cutscene. Or prevent me from wasting resources at the very least. I wish there was a way to check what the buff icons meant too. A lot of times I'm not certain what Formation Nichol has used. They also shouldn't make you have tents. Tent cost is a rounding error rapidly, it's just pointless.

Did a cool thing with Rain where I used the ability to take all hits for all allies and then he countered the boss four times. The balance of this game does seem a little weird. Like I can't imagine not using Fina and Lasswell for the most part, and Rain is the primary protagonist so it seems like he has to be in too. 

![[20261027125305_1.jpg]]

*Honestly? Ignis isn't a bad dude.*

Ignis is at least a pretty cool dude. I am interested in seeing where Lazarov and the Sworn Six's goals diverged. Also the Prince Shera and his dad thing seemed pretty predictable. I don't know why we're raising the rebel flag at the top of the castle though. The imperial succession is intact, I assume they're not changing everything about Zoldaad. 

![[20261027162259_1.jpg]]

*Lazarov, on the other hand, sucks.*

The resolution with Lid and Evan is really good and also cements Jake as basically the best. Lazarov remains a huge dick. 

Spent a bunch of time afterwards cleaning up a bunch of side questies, getting Leviathan and Ifrit, the latter of whom was the first real challenging fight I had. He beat me twice before I got him, he hits really hard. I think I did them out of order as I did the sidequests before getting the airship, which was good. Leviathan was very helpful in helping beat Ifrit. 

![[20261027221007_1.jpg]]

*The most challenging fight so far.*

Speaking of, the airship is pretty cool! Good digs. Extra friends. Oh, speaking of extra friends, my sidequesting got me Aileen, Wilhelm, and Camille. I also finished off the first tier of the colosseum and got the hammer, so I can get Titan soon. Overall, the sidequesting has been a nice little break from the main quest, I have one more to wrap up with Lid, and then I'll be ready to head on to Mysidia. 

![[20261027224038_1.jpg]]

*Stylish AND spacious.*

Earlier I said I found it a bit overwhelming but now I am enjoying the visions system a lot. I just mushed a bunch of guides together and took a bit of an average of the consensus and have been following that to lower the choice paralysis. No, you know what? I'm enjoying this whole game a lot. It's very charming and laid back and wonder what the ceiling of that is. Does something have to be revolutionary to get an A grade? Or does execution just get you there?
