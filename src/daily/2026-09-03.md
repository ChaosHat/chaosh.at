## chrono-trigger
Too many fights here in the end game dungeon. I've also definitely settled on Crono, Robo, and Ayla as my main 3. 

I do like how Lavos just seems to be some kind of entity. It's not a dark demon with goals. It's just some kind of...animal that wants to become the "perfect life" or whatever?

Also it's Cell?

If we're so worried about Robo not being made in the future he could just hang out here. Frog too! 

I got what the wiki says is ending 1B. It's a very good ending. 

This game holds up. I think compared to it's contemporaries and even now, it does a good job at a lot of things. This has to be the best turn based combat on the SNES, it and Super Mario RPG. The characters exude personality and are easy to keep straight. They each have very clear lanes that communicate a lot very quickly, which is really important in this era.

## split-fiction
Played some of this tonight with Nate since D&D was off and we had the free time anyways. 

My opinion of this game is roughly the same. It's cute. It's fun. It doesn't have a ton of mechanical depth, it's really getting by on being something you do with friends and by keeping it really varied, ideally before you get bored. It's honestly kind of like a co-op Mario Party in that regard. It just keeps changing what you're doing and being chaotic.
