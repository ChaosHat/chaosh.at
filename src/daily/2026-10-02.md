I think Dietrich's route might be hitting me at the wrong time. The actual benefit (the combat art improvement) is likely the power I would use the most in gameplay. The way you go about it is *the worst though*. Oh, more random skirmish battles and dungeon time? *Negative fun.* If this was my first or second playthrough? Okay maybe!

I think I've stumbled upon my exact issue with this game. It's as though they looked at all the people who said 3H was tactics Persona and said okay, how can we do that more and win those people over and they decided their missing ingredient was Mementos when that's *easily the worst part of Persona 5*.
