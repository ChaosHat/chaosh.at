## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2026-11-19 12.44.26.webp]]

*You're on Tifa.*

I'm on a boat and what do you do when you're on a boat? You play in a TCG tournament of course. You know, I've been on multiple cruises and none of them have had this. This would have been an improvement to all of them. The matches were all fairly easy but still fun. I only had to restart one against Madam M due to a minor lapse. 

I really enjoy how the game alternates the open world chapters with the more linear ones. It's a nice time to go on cruise control for a minute and follow the path without getting bogged down. 

![[2026-11-19 12.55.59.webp]]

*My deck of choice.*

I've said a lot of people are the worst but I'm pretty sure Hojo is actually the worst. I also get that Captain Titov was driven to this (partially because Hojo is the worst) but damn that's a cold thing to do to those men. We also get to see how Yuffie gets to Costa del Sol for a brief moment. I wonder why they slow roll adding her to the party for so long. 

![[2026-11-19 13.52.09.webp]]

*Sorry Barret, it's time for the obligatory beach episode.*

I love any time I can fight a Jenova, it's one of my favorite themes in the series. The way it combines that spacey otherworldliness and that intense, driving beat. With her down and Sephiroth messing with our minds a bit more before bailing, we're off to Costa del Sol for fun in the sun!

On the beach, I love riding this stupid segway. Severe GOB Bluth vibes here. I also think Johnny's Beachside Shack, with a little love, would be a pretty top tier location. Sure it's not literally on the beach but it's a short walk and it's pretty secluded. We should invest. 
## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-19 14.50.23.webp]]

So this game goes pretty hard emotionally from the jump. This did a really good job of making the characters instantly compelling. You feel for the kid because of his shitty mom. His dad seemed so nice and caring and then he's gone. It feels like the start of a prestige tv show.

And then you're chasing down a dude selling animal porn calling you a cumstain. Some whiplash there. 

Alright this game is pretty charming. I love a guy who thinks about Dragon Quest this much. Ichiban also seems like a nice guy, your traditional scoundrel with a heart of gold. Maybe also a little hot headed and dumb but still, that adds to the endearing nature. I like his friend and the young master too. It makes you feel empathy for the characters very effectively. 

If I were to complain I'd say the cutscene to actual gameplay ratio is pretty bad, but it's all pretty compelling so I haven't minded so far. 
## Final Fantasy VII
![[Screenshot_20261119-211433.webp]]

Sunk about another hour into this one, getting through the second reactor, falling through the church, and making it to Wall Market. It's funny, this game has its own quiet charm and it's doing a lot with what it can do, but I think there are obvious reasons why the Remake trilogy is the version of this story I really felt like I could fall in love with. I came to this game late, playing the PC port at some point in the early/mid 2000s when I was in high school and I thought it was perfectly fine to pretty good. 

The Remake trilogy adds a *lot* though. The music is so much more sweeping and beautiful. The performances both by the voice actors and the animators add so much depth. The writing is better, or it's at least conveyed better in a format where tone can matter. 

![[Screenshot_20261119-214038.webp]]

I still like this, in fact I think I like it *more* now because it is seeing them how they were, when they were there. It has the feeling of looking through old yearbooks. But I think everyone is peaking now, not when I was in high school.
