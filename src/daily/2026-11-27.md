## Yakuza: Like a Dragon
![[2026-11-27 11.37.01.webp]]

*Putting his life on the line for the benefit of all, again.*

The dungeon after fleeing Mabuchi's lair is so insanely long. Every zone change I'm like "surely this is the exit." The section in on is also shockingly far between save points which is annoying. 

Also I now know why you might not want to open a safe, sometimes there are people in them! I mean, the rewards are too high so you always want to open them anyways. Oh finally realized how to actually let the bond increase further. So much wasted bond. 

Eventually finished off that dungeon, ended the chapter and found out it's basically Mementos? We'll see how much I care. One thing I have really enjoyed about this is how non-obligatory a lot of side stuff has felt. 

![[2026-11-27 23.19.12.webp]]

*You got it kid.*

That's what I've devoted basically all of chapter seven to so far. Subquests, business management, getting my bond up. Some sweet subquests involving some little kids going through tough times. Also discovered Dragon Kart which is insane that they included it here but I don't think it holds up compared to more dedicated kart games so I don't think I'll be diving deep.

I am getting a little itch of the need to push forward main story wise, but this feels like actually what Fortune's Weave should have been. The ambition of this game is off the charts but it feels much more well executed. 

I've been playing just this mostly because I've been traveling for Thanksgiving and this runs better on the Steam Deck than Rebirth. It's going to be hard to reintegrate Rebirth into my play cycle since I'm on such a roll with Yakuza. And also I have to decide if I feel like rolling directly into Infinite Wealth right after. So many games, so little time.
