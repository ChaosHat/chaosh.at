## poplc
This game is pretty fun! I might actually try to play it alongside Belmont's Curse? As I say that, that does seem vaguely insane. I might just nurture it here or there to keep it live so later on I don't feel like I need to totally start over. We'll see.

The platforming and puzzles are consistently fun and make you feel great when you do them but also aren't so harsh that I get angry at it. Somehow this hits a very sweet spot of it takes me a couple tries and then I get it. And I'm aware that might be exactly for my band of skill, there are worse people getting angry and better people who think this is way too easy. But for me? Doing stuff like using the chakram to move platforms calling it back midair to move them, and then shooting something else to make the platforms appear? Just fun. 

I like the amulet system, this kind of stuff is always fun to be able to customize and pick your own playstyle and items or weapons of emphasis. I got this ability where you can leave a shadow and warp back to it. That's where the platforming might get kind of mind melting. 

I don't love the unbeatable boss battle (also this game got incredibly anime during that) but it was fun. Although maybe it was beatable if you're very good but if that's the case I wish it was easier to replay it. Also that dude who chases you down and imprisons you? That's an EMMI. You can't fool me. 
## sovereign
This game is actually kind of hard and I feel like I need to go back and understand the mechanics here and how it works. This is also the most interesting time travel/rewind mechanic in any game. 

There's a tension here where I don't want to use it because well, it's a demon. That can't be good. But the game also kind of wants you to use it. There's plot and mechanics and twists to it. Also, it's not just a rewind and do it again with more knowledge. There's some of that, but then it also throws curveballs at you. 

I got to a point where I had lost someone and I wanted to rewind to get them back because I had a lot of quests that required two knights, so having an odd number was really constraining my output. So I went ahead and did that and then a couple days later ||I found out there was a traitor in my midst who had murdered another one of my knights!|| So of course I was torn between...do I do it again to fix it? Or is this a consequence, is it balancing itself out? 

Of course, the demon popped by to taunt me. At first I thought it was going to claim credit for it, but no it was offering it's services. If only I could go back in time with foreknowlege of what's going to happen, I could avert this crisis. So now I kind of feel like I need/want to, just because hey, can I catch this traitor in advance? But what other calamity will befall me?
## lazarus
I've been just putting a few minutes here or there for a while and decided I wanted to play it a bit more to use this new Pokedaisy app that someone made that adds dual screen support on the Thor. It's really nice. It also adds a widescreen mod and it does things on the second screen like tell you what the other Pokemon is weak to which is so nice because there are so many Pokemon and I'm always like "oh yeah it's also this." It's just nice to not have to remember. 

I beat the first gym and I've captured some pretty cool Pokemon so I feel good about it now.
