## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2909400_20261109112637_1.png]]

*Size isn't everything Aerith.*

We have left Kalm. I didn't have enough money to buy the stuff from the shop there, so I'm hoping/assuming I'll get to go back? I don't remember, it's been a while. I did play some Queen's Blood while in there, man it's so fun. I really can't believe Square didn't spin this off into its own phone game or something. 

![[2909400_20261109111306_1.png]]

*Checkmate!*

The game looks so different from Remake, just the lushness and green everywhere. I think something is lost in the split between the two games with it though. The contrast isn't as immediate. 

I got through finding the chocobo and everything at the ranch, then I started on the Chadley of it all, who I'm not super enthused to meet again. Chadley is kind of the ur trap for me in this game. I wanted to focus on the more joyful things, and not as much busywork but the Chadley stuff is so good. I do want all the materia he has yes please, and I have open/extra slots for the materia. 

![[2909400_20261109135526_1.png]]

*This nerd again?*

But on the other hand, when I think back to the issues I had with this game the first time I tried to play, it's that it's so easy to get wrapped up in this stuff, and then you have a whole play session and you haven't advanced anything of value story wise. And having done some more of it now, it does feel more mandatory. 

Depending on how this goes, I might just resume my PS5 save I left at whatever way through. I always dislike coming back to an old save in a big, long game like this, it always feels so hard to get your bearings back, but you know what, I already did so much of this open world stuff and I'm not tooooootally sure I want to have to redo it.
## Sovereign Tower
Okay, I will say at this point it feels like act three drags. They're laying out little hints of how to turn back time to get a specific ending, I'm assuming you have to get the golden key without using any time rewinding. That seems maybe slightly wrong to me because the time traveling and the plot relevance seems like an important hook of the game, so to eschew it in a true ending seems a little wrong. I could see tactical usage. But we'll see. 

But overall the loop kind of loses its novelty. You can get new weapons and horses and all that but it's not fundamentally different.
