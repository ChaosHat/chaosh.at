## Final Fantasy VII Rebirth
![[2909400_20261107150438_1.png]]

*Who finds this fun?*

Finished chapter one. It's a good chapter to get it started off. It's fun that you get to play as Sephiroth and that they bothered to build out such a move set for the character. They could very easily have made it something you don't get to control. One thing that's not fun, that this game and the prior do in certain spots, are these little mini games like the Mako vacuum to open the elevators. It's just busy work. Why bother?

![[2909400_20261107160336_1.png]]

*Sephiroth is having a bad day.*

Another thing I dislike is scenarios where you're essentially in a cutscene that you have to go through very slowly manually. So when Cloud gets hurt and he has his limp and you're just painstakingly walking, and then having to do the QTEs to crawl around. It's just so slow and tedious.
## Sovereign Tower
Picking this back up, I'm going to try to use it as my quick get in, get out game. It's light and easy, the turns are short enough, so it should be doable. My only fear/problem with that usually those games require being 

I'm going to put some spoilers here.

![[Screenshot_20261107-223731.png]]

*Dulahan is clearly a totally normal human.*

So, one point of frustration, I went back in time to undo the murder and had to do it a couple times. I went back, immediately, expecting to be able to catch the person in the act. I knew when it happened after all, I should be able to ambush them. When it happened again, the demon admonished me for doing the same thing, I was supposed to do something else. 

So I went back *again* thinking I had missed some opportunity to prevent it. I painstakingly checked every room again at every turn for something I had missed somewhere. The day came again and someone was again murdered and I was told off by the demon.

What I realized was that it expected me to go forward to uncover the culprit, THEN go back in time to prevent it. I'm not sure how I was expected to know that based on what it told me and how the game had treated me. Usually just going back in time as soon as it happens is enough to unlock whatever alternate stuff there is. 

The funny thing is, at the end of it, I decided to let it ride anyways. I was kind of tired of replaying the same few days. I know how Phil Connors felt I guess.
