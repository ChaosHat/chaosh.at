## fefw
I'm putting this one lower just because slightly spoilery.

The mechanics of part two are so good it makes me angry that it's not like this earlier. I think regenerating your weapon durability for free and topping off your consumables lets them design the game a little tighter knowing these things will be at your disposal. 

Some fight spoilers below for part 2. 

So I beat part two, chapter six, part one (no typos there). This one was kind of a slog but unnecessarily so. I also beat the second part by absolutely cheesing it with Leda. I gave her the ability that lets her walk through opposing units, spammed Aurora's blessing, and drank the potion that lets her ignore movement penalties. She just solo waltzed on down. In Shadows also helped her be tanky. 

I finished the first fight for part 3, which I would say again was on the grueling side but I did have a little cheer when I saw the people come back. An extremely Avengers Endgame moment. 

In chapter 2 they give you all the shit back and god damn I'm not sure I'm ready for that. It's a lot of book keeping and gathering people up. There's also some more SECRET SCENES which, oh boy. There's a Leda one which wraps up her character development that all happens off screen apparently. She just tells you about it which is real bad.
