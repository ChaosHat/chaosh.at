## fefw
The paralogues are so good and they made me smile the whole time with how interesting and inventive they are. Some of the best fights this game has to offer so far and I just wish there were more. 

For all my gripes, I think my biggest one is that I can't recruit Anatolia. 

I'm getting close to turning off combat animations to speed it up. This game never totally recaptured the charm of the pixel art versions.

Ending a battle with the turn based boss mode is a cool idea...if the turn based jrpg battle thing was more interesting.

The restart chapter button really shouldn't be so close to the battle one...

Alright, we're on Cai's final chapter. Initially I had thought to play Leda next but now I'm not sure! I might want to save her for even later, when she'll be *even more powerful*. Bwahaha.
