This game has so many good ideas but I just didn't love the execution of it overall. The early levels are just too flat and linear and there's just not enough actual castle (although this could be mitigated by making it a castle by committee). I also wish there was a radial menu to be able to switch between more loadouts (obviously not doable on original hardware). The idea to have a weaknesses system is good, I just spent too much time switching in menus.
